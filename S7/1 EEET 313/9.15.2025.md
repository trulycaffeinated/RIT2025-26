# Non-Ideal Components and Resonance

The impedance of an ideal capacitor
$$
Z_c = \frac{1}{j\omega C} = \frac{1}{j2\pi f C}
$$
**Resonance** - Capacitance and inductance cancel each other out

Quality of capacitor $Q_c$
$$
Q_c = \frac{R_p}{X_c}
$$
Ideal inductor
$$
Z_l = j \omega L = j 2 \pi f L
$$
Quality of Inductor
$$
Q_l = \frac{Xl}{Rs} = \frac{2\pi f L}{R_s}
$$
