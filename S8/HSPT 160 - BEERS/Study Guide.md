# 🍺 **Beer Basics**

- **4 ingredients:** Water, malt (barley/grains), hops, yeast
- **Reinheitsgebot (1516):** Beer purity law (water, barley, hops; yeast added later)
- **Adjuncts:** Non-traditional ingredients
    - Good → enhance flavor
    - Bad → cheapen beer

---

# 🍻 **Ale vs Lager**

- **Ales:** Top-fermenting, 50–70°F
- **Lagers:** Bottom-fermenting, 45–55°F
- **Color ≠ type** (comes from malt)

---

# 🌿 **Hops**

- Functions:
    - Bitterness
    - Aroma
    - Preservation
- **Part used:** Female flower/cone
- **Acids:**
    - Alpha → bitterness (heat activated)
    - Beta → aroma/preservation
- **Noble hops:** Hallertau, Saaz, Spalt, Tettnang
- **Timing:**
    - Early → bitterness
    - Mid → flavor
    - Late → aroma

---

# 🧪 **Brewing Process**

- **Fermentation:** Yeast → sugar → alcohol + CO₂
- **Wort:** Liquid from mashed grains
- **Malting stages:** Steeping → Germination → Kilning
- **Dry hopping:** Adds aroma without bitterness
- **Boil purpose:**
    - Sterilizes wort
    - Activates hop bitterness
- **Most important factor:** Cleanliness/sanitation
- **Hops added to:** Balance malt sweetness

---

# 🍺 **Beer Styles**

- **Kölsch:** Ale yeast, lager-style brewing
- **Trappist:** Brewed in monastery under monks (profits to monastery/charity)
- **Lambic:** Wild/spontaneous fermentation, sour
    - ≥30% unmalted wheat
    - Aged/dried hops
- **Gruit:** No hops, uses herbs/spices
- **Rauchbier:** Smoked malt
- **Bock:** Strong (~7% ABV), malty lager, spring
- **Export Stout:** Stronger stout for travel
- **Steam Beer (California Common):** Lager yeast, warm temps
- **Altbier:** German ale

### Ale vs Lager Styles

- Ale: IPA, Stout, Wheat Beer, Barleywine
- Lager: Pilsner, Bock, Steam/California Common

---

# 🌍 **History & Geography**

- **Origin of beer:** Mesopotamia (~10,000 BCE)
- **Reinheitsgebot:** Bavaria, 1516
- **Oldest brewery:** Weihenstephan Abbey
- **Oldest US brewery:** Yuengling
- **Most beer per capita:** Czech Republic
- **Largest hop producer:** Germany
- **Refrigeration → year-round lagers**
- **IPA purpose:** Preserve beer for long voyages
- **Most common lager:** Pilsner

# 🍺 **Flavor & Characteristics**

- **Color:** From malt/grain
- **Dark beer flavors:** Coffee, chocolate, roasted
- **Big beers:** High ABV, heavy, intense

---

# 🍽️ **Food & Pairing**

- **Most versatile beer:** Wheat beer
- **“10 or 2 rule”:** Cook <10 min or >2 hours

---

# 🍹 **Beer Variations**

- **Shandy:** Beer + soda
- **Radler:** Beer + fruit juice

---

# 🧊 **Storage & Quality**

- **Main threats:** Heat, light, age
- **Lightstruck:** Skunky flavor from light
- **Best packaging:** Cans (block light + oxygen)

---

# 🍷 **Alcohol & Body**

- **ABV:** Alcohol by volume
- **IBU:** Bitterness measurement
- **Alcohol absorbed in:** Small intestine
- **Processing rate:** ~1 drink/hour
- **Legal BAC:** 0.08
- **BAC factors:** Rate, body size, gender, food, carbonation, age, meds

# 🧠 **Core Concepts**

- **Imperial/Double:** Higher alcohol version

# 🧩 **High-Yield Test Concepts**

- Malt = main fermentable sugar
- Darker malt → darker, more roasted flavor
- Hops = bitterness + aroma + preservation
- Beer served too cold → muted flavor
