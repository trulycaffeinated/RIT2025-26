Question - What are the four basic ingredients in beer according to the Reinheitsgebot?  
Answer - Water, yeast, grains, and hops

Question - What is an adjunct in beer, and how does it differ under German purity laws?  
Answer - An adjunct is any ingredient added to beer beyond the four basic ingredients and is not allowed under German purity laws

Question - On the standard beer color scale (0–8), what number corresponds to black beer?  
Answer - 8

Question - What style of beer is characterized by the addition of citrus and coriander?  
Answer - Belgian Witbier

Question - What is a key visual characteristic expected of lagers?  
Answer - They should be crystal clear

Question - Which beer style is known for strong coffee, roasted, and dark chocolate flavors?  
Answer - Stout

Question - What flavor/aroma characteristics are commonly associated with IPAs?  
Answer - Citrus, grapefruit, pine, bitterness, and tropical fruit notes

Question - What is a Schwarzbier, and what are its typical characteristics?  
Answer - A black lager with roasted, coffee-like, and chocolate flavors

Question - What type of beer is a Helles?  
Answer - A pale German lager

Question - What are common flavor characteristics of Belgian strong ales?  
Answer - Sweet, fruity, spicy, and alcoholic warmth

Question - What distinguishes a Saison from other beer styles?  
Answer - Fruity, spicy, sometimes funky flavors with a dry finish

Question - What is a Radler, and what is its defining characteristic?  
Answer - A beer mixed with fruit soda, usually citrus, and is low alcohol

Question - What are typical flavor notes found in Irish Red Ales?  
Answer - Caramel, molasses, and a balance of sweetness and bitterness

Question - What is a key difference between ales and lagers in fermentation?  
Answer - Ales use top-fermenting yeast at warmer temperatures while lagers use bottom-fermenting yeast at cooler temperatures

Question - What sensory characteristics might indicate a lightstruck beer?  
Answer - Skunky or chemical aroma or flavor
