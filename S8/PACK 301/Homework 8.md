**1. Why do you think it is important to inform customers that the packaging has changed, although the product is the same? (15 points)**
Packaging is a part of brand recognition. If the packaging suddenly looks different customers may think its the wrong product or that something about the product has changed. Communicating and informing consumers about packaging changes prevents confusion and bad reviews while hopefully maintaining trust.

**2.**
**a.) From an in-store standpoint, it’s not that big of a deal when the packaging changes - explain why (10 points); and** 
In store - not a big deal. Shoppers can physically see and examine a product before making the decision to purchase. Reading labels to ensure the product is correct or correct for them.

**b.) yet, packaging changes are "a big deal online" - explain why (10 points).**
Online - a big deal. Shoppers rely heavily on images and text in descriptions / drop downs to ensure the product is what they expect it to be. If packaging looks any different than they expect they may not think its the right product, or alternatively think they received the wrong product.

**3. There are three ways for on-line retailers to communicate a packaging update on a digital product page.  Explain each (10 points each = 30 points total).**

**a.) Product Title** - adding phrases like "new packaging" or "an update look" in the title

**b.) Gallery images** - showing clear images of the new packaging and a possible old vs new comparison

**c.) Feature bullets** - a clear statement typically saying new package same product
