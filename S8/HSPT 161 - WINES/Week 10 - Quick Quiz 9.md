## Sparking and Champagne
Did Champagne producers invent sparking wine? If not, who did? :united
**No, the English invented it**

What is the third grape allowed in Champagne production, but is often considered less noble than Chardonnay and Pinot Noir?  
**Pinot Meunier**

What is dosage? How is it different from disorgement?  
**Dosage is the addition of sugar/wine after disgorgement to adjust sweetness; disgorgement is the removal of dead yeast from the bottle**

Which is drier - extra dry or brut?  
**Brut**

What is the least expensive method - traditional or Charmat? Why?  
**Charmat, because the secondary fermentation happens in large tanks instead of individual bottles**

What is the method required for Cava production? Where is it made?  
**Traditional method; Spain**

Which country produces and likes good Sekts?  
**Germany**

Name the grape used in the production of spumante from Asti? Which country?  
**Moscato (Muscat Blanc); Italy**

Why did the French start producing sparking wine in California and other countries?  
**To expand production and take advantage of suitable climates while increasing profits and market reach**

What makes Champagne and sparking wines excellent with a variety of food?  
**High acidity and carbonation cleanse the palate and pair well with many dishes**
