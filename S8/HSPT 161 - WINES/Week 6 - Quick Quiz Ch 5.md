1. Name one Oregon AVA
   **Willamette Valley**
2. What is Oregon's wine nickname?
   **Pinot Ville**
3. Describe the climate conditions in Willamette Valley.
   **Cool Climate region - long growing seasons, wet winters dry summers**
4. Oregon's Willamette Valley is often compared to what French wine region?
   **Burgundy (Bourgogne)**
5. Name the key red grape in Oregon.
   **Pinot Noir**
6. Describe the effects the Cascade Mountains have on these two states
   **Creates a rain shadow effect. Oregon is cool wet, maritime climate, Washington is dry sunny continental climate**
7. Name one Washington AVA
   **Columbia Valley**
8. Describe the climate conditions in Columbia Valley.
   **Dry, desert-like climate**
9. What can be done in a Washington vineyard to prevent grapes from becoming sunburnt and unbalanced for wine production?
   **Canopy Management, careful row orientation, avoid overexposing to light**
10. Name the red grape that first made Washington famous in the modern era.
    **Merlot**
