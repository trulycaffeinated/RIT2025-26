1. What is the very popular red grape of Australia?

2. What can viticulturists do in a vineyard to prevent grapes from becoming sunburned?
   **Canopy management - maintain leaf cover for shade**
3. During harvest, in a very hot climate, what can be done to ensure the grapes are in good condition when they arrive at the winery for processing?
   **Harvest at night or early morning**
4. What U.S. state does Australia's wine history resemble? Why?

5. Do the Australians follow traditional or innovative practices?
   **Innovative**
6. Name the white grape that New Zealand is famous for producing?
   **Sauvignon Blanc**
7. Are New Zealand's wines generally more expensive than Australia's? If so, why?
   **Yes**
8. What is the New Zealand red grape that is gaining international popularity?
   **Pinot Noir**
9. How is New Zealand able to achieve riper fruit flavors than other cool climate regions?
