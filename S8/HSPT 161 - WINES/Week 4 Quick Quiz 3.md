## What is a Terroir?
French word for "sense"/place of where grapes are grown, specifically all the factors relating to the environment in which a grape is grown and how it affects the wine

## Will Adding sugar to a tank of fermenting wine guarantee a sweet wine in the end?
No, adding sugar does not guarantee a sweet wine, as it typically just gives yeast more fuel leading to a potentially higher alcohol level by ripening the grape.

## Why Should we avoid adding alcohol to wine after fermentation is complete?
It can throw the wine out of balance, mask terroir and varietal characters, and can make the wine less refined.
