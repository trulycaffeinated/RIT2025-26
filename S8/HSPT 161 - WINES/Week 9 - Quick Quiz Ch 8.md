Argentina, South Africa, and Chile

Which Country had major influence on the Chilean wine industry?  
**France**
What is the unique red grape of Chile that was misidentified for years?  
**Carménère**
What is the unique white grape of Argentina?  
**Torrontés**
Name the major red grape of Argentina that is usually bottled varietally, but is more often seen globally in blends?  
**Malbec**
What is Argentina's major wine producing region?  
**Mendoza**
How can Argentina produce good wines from grapes planted so far away from large bodies of water?  
**High altitude vineyards in the Andes Mountains provide large temperature swings and irrigation from snowmelt**
Phylloxera has not affected which of these three countries?  
**Chile**
What is the white workhorse grape of South Africa?  
**Chenin Blanc**
What is the cross red grape created for South Africa's terroir?  
**Pinotage**
How many years has South Africa been exporting their wines?  
**Since the early 1990s (approximately 30+ years)**
