To perform modulation we need to multiply electrical signals together somehow, we can do this with non-linear devices.

A diode for example has a non-linear IV curve
$$
\begin{aligned}
I = I_0^{(e^{\frac{qV}{nKT}}-1)} \\
I = c_0 + c_1V + c_2V^2 + c_3V^3 + ... \\
\end{aligned}
$$
