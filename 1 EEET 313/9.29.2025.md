## Matching Networks
We need to find the series reactance that transforms the series resistance into the desired parallel resistance
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q = \frac{X_L}{R_S} \\
\\
R_P = (Q^2+1)R_S \\
\\
X_P = \frac{R_P}{Q} = (1+\frac{1}{Q^2})X_S
\end{aligned}
$$
