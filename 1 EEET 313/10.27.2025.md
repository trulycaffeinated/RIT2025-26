## AM Demodulation
**Product Detector**
Multiplies the AM signal back down to audio spectrum
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Carrier ------------------- Lower Sideband ------------------Upper Sideband}\\
V_{am}(t) = V_csin(\omega_ct) + (\frac{V_m}{2})cos(2\pi(f_c-f_m)t)-(\frac{V_m}{2})cos(2\pi(f_c+f_m)t)
\end{aligned}
$$
**Envelope Detector** - strip information off of the envelope
Removes the information from the envelope of the signal

Starting with carrier term (Product Detector)
$$
\begin{aligned}
V_csin(\omega_ct) \\
\text{carrier is multipled by sin at carrier f} \\
\frac{V_c}{2}cos(\theta) + \frac{V_c}{2}cos(2\omega_ct) \\
\\
\text{lower sideband} \\
\text{LS multipled by a sin at the carrier frequency} \\
\frac{V_m}{4}sin(2\omega_c-\omega_m)+\frac{V_m}{4}sin(\omega_mt) \\
\\
\text{signal at modulation freq (pulled from above)} \\
\frac{V_m}{4}sin(\omega_mt) \\
\\
\text{USB} \\
\frac{V_m}{4}sin(2\omega_c+\omega_m)+\frac{V_m}{4}sin(-\omega_mt) \\
\\
\text{modulating signal (pulled from above)} \\
\frac{V_m}{4}sin(-\omega_mt) \\
\end{aligned}
$$
