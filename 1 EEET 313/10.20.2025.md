Modulation Equation
$$
\begin{aligned}
E_{am}(t) = (E_c + E_i sin(2\pi f_m t)) \times sin(\omega_ct) \\
\end{aligned}
$$
Percentage of modulation
$$
\begin{aligned}
m = \frac{E_i}{E_c}=\frac{V_{peak}-V_{valley}}{V_{peak}+V_{valley}}
\end{aligned}
$$


Information in the USB (Upper Side Band) - your desired signal
Information in the LSB (Lower Side Band) - Frequency flipped signal

Both contain the same information, LSB is just flipped

Information in the Carrier $F_c$ - Nothing, nada, zip

**Why do we need both? Why do we need the carrier?** 

We can improve the efficiency of AM, we can remove the carrier and just transmit the sidebands
Double Sideband Suppressed Carrier
- (DSB-SC)

You can also remove the LSB - so all power goes into the USB.
Single Sideband (SSB)
- Either sideband can be transmitted.
