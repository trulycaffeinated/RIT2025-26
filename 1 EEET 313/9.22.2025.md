# LC Filters Cont.
Design a butterworth filter with the following parameters...

| Lab Section | Filter R0 | Corner f (MHz) | 50dB frequency (MHz) |
| ----------- | --------- | -------------- | -------------------- |
| 1           | 50        | 2.3            | 9                    |
| 2&3         | 50        | 1.6            | 6.5                  |
Lab section 1...
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Lab Section 1} \\
\frac{f}{f_c} = \frac{9\text{ MHz}}{2.3\text{ MHz}}=n = 3.9 \approx 4\text{ = (Order of filter)} \\
\\
\text{Lab Section 2} \\
\frac{f}{f_c} = \frac{6.5\text{ MHz}}{1.6\text{ MHz}}=n = 4.1 \approx 4\text{ = (Order of filter)} \\
\end{aligned}
$$
For the filter...
$$
\begin{aligned}
L_{ref}\\
\\
L_{ref}=\frac{R_{ref}}{2\pi f_c} = \frac{50}{2\pi\times2.3\times10^6}=3.46\times10^{-6} \\
C_{ref} \\
\\
C_{ref} = \frac{1}{2\pi R_0 f_c} = \frac{1}{2\pi \times 50 \times 2.3 \times 10^6} = 1.38\times10^{-9}
\\
\text{Component calculations} \\
\text{The C1, C2, etc. come from normalized filter values} \\
C_1 \\
C_1 = C_{ref}\times C_1 = 1.38\times10^{-9} \times 0.765 = 1.05nF\\
L_2 \\
L_2 = L_{ref}\times L_2 = 3.46\times10^{-6} \times 1.848 = 6.39\micro H\\
C_3 \\
C_3 = C_{ref}\times C_3 =1.38\times10^{-9}  \times 1.848 = 2.55nF\\
L_4 \\
L_4 = L_{ref}\times L_4 = 3.46\times10^{-6} \times 0.765 = 2.64\micro H\\
\end{aligned}
$$
