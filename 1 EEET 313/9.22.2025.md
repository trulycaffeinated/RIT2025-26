# LC Filters Cont.
Design a butterworth filter with the following parameters...

| Lab Section | Filter R0 | Corner f (MHz) | 50dB frequency (MHz) |
| ----------- | --------- | -------------- | -------------------- |
| 1           | 50        | 2.3            | 9                    |
| 2&3         | 50        | 1.6            | 6.5                  |
Lab section 1...
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Lab Section 1} \\
\frac{f}{f_c} = \frac{9\text{ MHz}}{2.3\text{ MHz}}=n = 3.9 \approx 4\text{ = (Order of filter)} \\
\\
\text{Lab Section 2} \\
\frac{f}{f_c} = \frac{6.5\text{ MHz}}{1.6\text{ MHz}}=n = 4.1 \approx 4\text{ = (Order of filter)} \\
\end{aligned}
$$
For the filter...
$$
\begin{aligned}
L_{ref}\\
\\
L_{ref}=\frac{R_{ref}}{2\pi f_c} = \frac{50}{2\pi\times2.3\times10^6}=3.46\times10^{-6} \\
C_{ref} \\
\\
C_{ref} = \frac{1}{2\pi R_0 f_c} = \frac{1}{2\pi \times 50 \times 2.3 \times 10^6} = 1.38\times10^{-9}
\\
\text{Component calculations} \\
\text{The C1, C2, etc. come from normalized filter values} \\
L_1 \\
L_1 = L_{ref}\times L_1 =  3.46\times10^{-6}\times 0.618 = 2.1\micro H\\
C_2 \\
C_2 = C_{ref}\times C_2 = 1.38\times10^{-9} \times 1.618 = 2.2nF\\
L_3 \\
L_3 = L_{ref}\times L_3 =3.46\times10^{-6} \times 2 = 6.9\micro H\\
C_4 \\
C_4 = C_{ref}\times C_4 = 1.38\times10^{-9}\times 1.618 = 2.2nF\\
L_5 \\
L_5 = L_{ref}\times L_5 = 3.46\times10^{-6} \times 0.618 = 2.1\micro H\\
\end{aligned}
$$

### PWB Build Calculations
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Calculate $L_{ref}$ from 22uH inductor for fifth order filter} \\
\text{L2 and L3 are the same for fifth order shunt}\\
L_{2/3} = L_{ref} \times 1.618\\
L_{ref}=\frac{L_{2/3}}{1.618}\\
L_{ref}=\frac{22\micro H}{1.618}\\
L_{ref}=1.359\micro\\
\\
\text{Calculate $f_c$ from $L_{ref}$ using 22uH inductor} \\
L_{ref}=\frac{R_{ref}}{2\pi f_c} \\
f_c = \frac{R_{ref}}{2\pi L_{ref}} \\
f_c = \frac{50 \ohm}{2\pi \times 22\micro}
f_c = 361 \text{ KHz} \\
\\
\text{Calculate Capacitor values using found Corner Freq} \\
C_{ref} = \frac{1}{2\pi R_0 f_c} = \frac{1}{2\pi \times 50 \times 361\times 10^6} = 8.8  \times 10^{-12} \\

\end{aligned}
$$
