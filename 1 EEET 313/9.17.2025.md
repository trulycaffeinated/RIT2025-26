### Simple RL High Pass Filter
Resistor Capacitor make a low pass filter (RC)
Resistor Inductor make a high pass filter (RL)

Combining the two can make a **bandpass** filter (RCL)
 
**Susceptance** - Complex part of the admittance
**Admittance** - $\frac{1}{X}$ 1 over impedance

$$
B_l = \frac{1}{X_L} = \frac{1}{2 \pi f L}
$$
$$
B_c = \frac{1}{X_C} = 2 \pi f C
$$
$$
Y=-jB_L + jB_c
$$
At resonance the inductive susceptance and capacitive susceptance are equal
