### Simple RL High Pass Filter
Resistor Capacitor make a low pass filter (RC)
Resistor Inductor make a high pass filter (RL)

Combining the two can make a **bandpass** filter (LC Tank Circuit)
 
**Susceptance** - Complex part of the admittance
**Admittance** - $\frac{1}{X}$ 1 over impedance

$$
B_l = \frac{1}{X_L} = \frac{1}{2 \pi f L}
$$
$$
B_c = \frac{1}{X_C} = 2 \pi f C
$$
$$
Y=-jB_L + jB_c
$$
At resonance the inductive susceptance and capacitive susceptance are equal
At resonance the current in both capacitor and inductor are equal but opposite directions. This creates very high impedance, and no current flows in from the source. This is a **tank circuit**

Calculate resonance frequency of tank circuit with R = 50 $\ohm$ L = 0.05$\mu$H and C = 25pF
$$
\begin{aligned}
Resonance \\
f_r=\frac{1}{2\pi \sqrt{(0.05\mu H)(25pF)}} = 142.3MHz \\
\\
Quality \\ 
Q = R/X_L = R/X_C \\

\\

\end{aligned}
